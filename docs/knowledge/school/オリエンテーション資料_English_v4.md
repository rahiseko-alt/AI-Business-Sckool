Kaiho Gakuen AI Business School / Student Handbook。全16枚、英語。各スライドのタイトルと要点（原文英語のまま）。

1. **For New Students — Orientation Materials**（表紙）
2. **Document Submission Checklist** — Submit and confirm these during orientation: Student Life Pledge / Financial Supporter Information Form / Enrollment Intent Confirmation / Part-Time Job Status Survey / Explanation and Consent for Residence Status Procedures / Personal Information Consent Form / Consent for SNS Photo Use. If anything is blank or missing, the school will check it.
3. **Student Life / School Rules** — Check the school rules in simple language; look at the pictures and remember the important points; if you do not understand, ask a teacher right away. Important: If you have a problem, do not deal with it alone. Ask for help.
4. **Study / Graduation** — You study at school for two years. Attend classes and earn credits. After graduation, you receive the title of Specialist. Aim to become a person who can use AI at work. Important: Goal: build the skills you need to work in Japan.
5. **Attendance / Being Late** — Attendance of **80% or more is required**. **90% or more is the goal**, if possible. Be in your seat before class starts. If you are absent or late, contact the school first. Important: Low attendance will make your visa renewal difficult.
6. **Part-Time Work Hours** — You need a residence card and school permission. Up to **8 hours per day, and within 28 hours in 7 days**. During long vacations: up to 8 hours per day, and within 40 hours in 7 days. If you change your part-time job, you must report it to the school. Important: Add together the hours from all of your jobs.
7. **Monthly Documents to Submit** — Payslip (copy OK) / Part-time job shift schedule / If possible: attendance record / time card / Proof of National Health Insurance payment (payment certificate or printed payment confirmation screen). If submission is late, consult the school first.
8. **Prohibited Jobs** — You cannot work at pachinko parlors, bars, or clubs. Even dishwashing or cleaning there is not allowed. Do not trust anyone who says "It is okay." If unsure, ask the school before you start. Caution: A prohibited job will lead to expulsion or return to your home country.
9. **School Manners** — Greet teachers and classmates. Come to school in clean clothes. **During class, use your phone only when the teacher instructs you.** Do not eat during class. Important: Your daily manners build your reputation in Japan.
10. **How to Use School Facilities** — Keep the restroom clean. Flush only toilet paper. Separate your trash. Do not smoke inside or around the school.
11. **Tuition / Consultation** — Pay tuition by the deadline. If payment may be late, tell the office in advance. After consultation, you can confirm how to pay. If you do not contact the school and do not pay, the school will take disciplinary action. Important: Do not decide money problems by yourself.
12. **Traffic / Bicycles** — Do not ride a bicycle while looking at your phone / after drinking alcohol. Ride in a single line. Do not make loud noise at night. Commuter pass: come to the office. Important: Accidents and violations are bad for both your visa and the school.
13. **School Rules / Discipline** — Wrongdoing → warning. Serious problems may lead to suspension. Violence, misconduct, and illegal work are prohibited. Before any disciplinary action, you have a chance to explain.
14. **Residence Card / Procedures** — Always carry your residence card. If you move, go to the city office within 14 days. Tell the school if your address or phone number changes. Join National Health Insurance and pay the premiums. Consult the school early about visa renewal.
15. **Insurance Check** — Join National Health Insurance; join bicycle insurance; check apartment fire insurance; ask the office if unclear. For bicycles, check liability coverage rather than compulsory auto liability insurance.
16. **Consultation / Emergency Contact** — Homeroom teacher: classes, grades, career path. Office: visas, tuition, certificates. You can also report bullying or harassment. Incidents/accidents: 110; fire/ambulance: 119. In an emergency, make sure you are safe first, then contact the school.
